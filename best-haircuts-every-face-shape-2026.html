Top 7 Best Haircuts for Every Face Shape in 2026: The Ultimate Proven Guide

The single most important step before any new haircut is knowing your face shape. Get that right and every cut on this list will work for you. The fastest way is a free online check: upload a selfie to a face shape detector and it tells you whether you are oval, round, square, heart, oblong or diamond in seconds.

1. Oval face: the long layered cut

Oval faces are the most balanced shape, so almost any length works. Long layers are the absolute safest choice because they keep that natural balance. See more ideas in this oval face hairstyles roundup.

2. Round face: the textured lob with a side part

A collarbone-length lob with a deep side part adds height and draws the eye down, making a round face look longer. Browse round face hairstyles for more options.

3. Square face: soft wispy layers

Soft layers around the jaw break up strong angles. Avoid blunt chin-length bobs. The square face hairstyles guide covers the best picks.

4. Heart face: the chin-length bob

A bob that ends near the chin adds width at the bottom and balances a wider forehead.

5. Oblong face: curtain bangs with volume

Bangs shorten the look of a long face, and volume at the sides adds width. Read more about oblong face hairstyles.

6. Diamond face: the side-swept pixie

Side-swept fringe softens wide cheekbones and fills out the forehead area.

7. For men: the textured crop or a buzz cut

Men with oval or square faces look sharp in a buzz cut. Before you commit, try a buzz cut filter on your own photo, and check the men's hairstyles by face shape chart.
